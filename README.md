### 🔭 Currently building: [SocialRum](https://socialrum.com)

- **AI script generator** — multi-turn chat that asks clarifying questions, writes in 7 languages, and personalises scripts from a creator's profile and voice
- **Creator analytics** — Instagram & YouTube analyzers with real posting-pattern charts, competitor tracking and AI niche detection
- **In-browser video editor** — auto-captions with AssemblyAI (including Hindi), caption styling, music sync and video export
- **Niche trend discovery** — news from five sources, matched to each creator's niche, one click from a script

### 🛠️ Open source: [deploycheck](https://github.com/peeyushp00-create/deploycheck)

A CLI and GitHub Action that catches the bugs that work on your laptop and break when you deploy — imports whose letter case fails on Linux, undocumented environment variables, and `.env` files exposed to git. Zero dependencies, auto-fix, tested on Windows, macOS and Linux.
